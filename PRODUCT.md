# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vensight serves founders, operators, analysts, and agencies researching companies. Contributors discover and analyze company URLs. Developers and administrators review results and manage the public directory.

## Product Purpose

Make company profiles easy to find, compare, and inspect, with clear source information and AI-assisted summaries. Authenticated tools support discovery, analysis, and editorial review without implying that generated content is verified fact.

## Brand Personality

Clear, composed, practical. The approved UKO Figma system and homepage prototype are the reference. This is a work-focused directory, with compact information and direct actions.

## Anti-references

No previous golden editorial theme, gradients, decorative charts, fabricated quality scores, repeating marketing-card trios, or oversized headings. Use the approved blue Geist V logo. Geist is the selected UI font; no italics or weights above bold.

## Design Principles

- Keep browsing and comparison public; make sign-in benefits explicit.
- Use measured Figma values and shared components across routes.
- Show real directory data and label seeded demo content.
- Preserve role boundaries and existing API behavior.
- Review a complete local implementation before deployment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts down to 320px, labeled forms, clear pending/error/empty states, and reduced-motion support are part of the approved design acceptance criteria.
